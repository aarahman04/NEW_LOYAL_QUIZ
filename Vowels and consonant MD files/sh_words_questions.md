## Notes / anomalies found in the source PDF

1. **Q1 has no printed number.**
2. **Page 94 is a stray page** — it contains only the page number, no question and no picture. It is skipped, so the 100 questions come from pages 1–93 and 95–101. Page 102 is blank.
3. **Printed numbering errors:** `12` is skipped entirely (page 11 is `11`, page 12 is `13`); `14` appears twice (pages 13, 14); `23` appears twice (pages 23, 24); and the printed sequence jumps from `91` (page 92) to `93` (page 93). Because of these, the printed number runs behind the real position for most of the file.
4. **No answer marked** on sequential #3, #8 and #90. Based on the pictures the intended answers are `ship`, `shy` and `shoe` respectively.
5. **Non-`sh` distractor:** `soup` is used as the B option on sequential #24, #25, #26, #60, #61, #62 and #72 — it isn't an `sh` word.
6. **Heavy repetition:** questions #37–#71 repeat the artwork and options of #1–#35 almost exactly, and #72–#77 repeat #62–#67 again. Roughly two thirds of the deck is duplicated content.
7. **Spacing artifacts** in the source text (e.g. `A.  she`, trailing spaces after options) have been cleaned up here.
