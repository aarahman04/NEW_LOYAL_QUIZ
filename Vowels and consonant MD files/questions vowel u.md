## Notes / anomalies found in the source PDF

These are problems in the original file, left uncorrected in the extraction:

1. **Q1 has no printed number** in the PDF.
2. **Numbering errors:** the printed numbers jump and repeat — `41` appears twice (pages 41, 42), `68` appears twice (pages 67, 68), numbers `55` and `59` are skipped, the sequence jumps from `69` straight to `80`, and the final printed number is `102` even though there are only 92 questions.
3. **Missing picture:** the page printed as `67` (sequential #66) has no image at all in the PDF, so there is no `66.png`. Its options are `sun / sum`.
4. **Picture/option mismatch:** the page printed as `2` (sequential #2) shows a **school bus** but offers `sun / sum`, with `sun` underlined. Same artwork is reused correctly later at sequential #65 (`bus / sum`).
5. **No answer marked:** sequential #27 (`dug / buy`) has no underlined option. The picture shows someone digging, so `dug` is the intended answer.
6. **Repeated content:** sequential #67–#92 largely repeat the artwork and options of #3–#48.
7. **Off-topic option:** sequential #39 offers `alum / hunt`, and #40 offers `alum / pump` — `alum` is not a Kg 3 level word.
8. **Sensitive image:** sequential #24 uses a picture of a handgun for the word `gun`.
